# See who is connected to this PC

A small tray icon for Windows that lists the SSH, RDP and WSL sessions currently open on the machine — and tells you when a new one starts.

## What it does

The icon shows the number of active remote sessions. Click it for the list: who is logged in, from where, and for how long. When a new session appears, Windows shows a notification.

Useful wherever several people — or autonomous AI agents — work on remote machines: a forgotten SSH or RDP session no longer stays open unnoticed.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, x64 or ARM64. Nothing to configure; start it and it sits in the tray.
